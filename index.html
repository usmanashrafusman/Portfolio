I have built backends across a pretty wide range of products: trading and payments platforms at Naqdi, a super app that handled food delivery and e-commerce at Zapp, digital banking infrastructure at Soneri Bank, in-venue food ordering at Nokia Arena, and a Canva-style content platform at CreateSmart. The work that sticks with me is always in the layer nobody talks about. The payment router, the order dispatch queue, the webhook that has to fire correctly every single time. That is where I spend most of my time, and where I know exactly what breaks if you cut corners.

I care about systems holding under pressure more than I care about moving fast. Both things are possible. I just refuse to sacrifice one for the other.
